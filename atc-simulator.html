QUICK START — phraseology & rules

Your job

  • Arrivals enter at an edge fix on a published arrival. Descend and sequence them, then clear an ILS. They must land.
  • Departures start their roll on a runway. Climb them to the next sector's release level, route them to their exit fix, hand them off.
  • Overflights cross the sector. Keep them separated, hand them off near the boundary.
  • An aircraft that crosses the boundary without a handoff is a sector bust.

Separation

  • Loss of separation = less than 5.0 NM laterally and less than 1000 ft vertically. Exactly 5.0 NM or exactly 1000 ft is legal.
  • On final, wake turbulence spacing applies instead: up to 8 NM for a light behind a super. Low-visibility procedures add 2 NM more.
  • Amber = predicted within 2 minutes. Red = happening now. Below 1000 ft above the field on final is the tower's problem.

The crews are not perfect

A clearance is checked the instant you issue it — a refusal is immediate and says why. Acting on it takes the crew a moment, and they may query it (“say again”), read it back wrong, or decline. Watch the readback line in the CONTROL tab. Turn this off with Crew response & error on the SECTOR tab.

Approach gate

An ILS clearance is only accepted inside 12 NM, within ±30° of runway heading, within 35° of the extended centreline, at or below the 3° glidepath and at 250 kt or less. Unstable at 3 NM, a runway still occupied at 2 NM, or no visual contact at the 200 ft decision height gets you a go-around.

Mouse & keyboard

  • Click a target or strip to select · Ctrl+click adds to a multi-selection · right-click opens a menu.
  • Shift+drag from a selected target to vector it · Shift+wheel steps its level.
  • Drag a data block to reposition it; double-click it to re-enable auto placement.
  • Drag arrival strips into the order you intend to land them — you are scored against your own plan.
  • Drag empty scope to pan, wheel to zoom, FIT to reset.
  • ` pause · Tab/Shift+Tab next/previous · Esc deselect · ↑/↓ history · Ctrl+Z undo · F1 this panel.
  • Focus off the command box: Space pause · S step · C jump to the conflict · H hints · R replay · 1 2 4 8 rate.

The four tabs

  • CONTROL — the selected aircraft and every clearance as a button.
  • DISPLAY — declutter, colour modes, altitude filter, halos, MSA grid, vector length, scope rotation, brightness, range/bearing and CPA tools, vertical profile, PNG export.
  • SECTOR — the briefing, coordination offers, flow restrictions, runway changes, realism switches, the AI controller, hot-seat positions and the scenario script.
  • ANALYSIS — live advisories with suggested resolutions, workload, a conflict heat map, route efficiency and the fuel cost of your vectoring.

Replay

The whole session is recorded as keyframes. Press R or REPLAY to scrub back, watch a conflict develop, then TAKE OVER HERE to carry on from that moment against the identical traffic. Each separation loss in the session summary has its own REPLAY button.
