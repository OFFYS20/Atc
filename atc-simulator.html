QUICK START — phraseology & rules

Your job

  • Arrivals enter at an edge fix at cruise. Descend and sequence them, then clear them for an ILS. They must land.
  • Departures start their roll on a runway. Climb them, route them to their exit fix, hand them off at or above FL100.
  • Overflights cross the sector. Keep them separated, hand them off near the boundary.
  • An aircraft that crosses the boundary without a handoff is a sector bust.

Separation

  • Loss of separation = less than 5.0 NM laterally and less than 1000 ft vertically. Exactly 5.0 NM or exactly 1000 ft is legal.
  • Amber = predicted conflict within 2 minutes on current vectors. Red = actual loss of separation, right now.
  • Aircraft below 1000 ft above the field on final are exempt (tower's problem).

Command line

Type CALLSIGN then one or more commands. With a target already selected the callsign is optional. Several commands may be chained: AAL123 D070 H270 S210. Case does not matter.

AAL123select that aircraft (no command = just select)
H270   HDG 270turn onto heading 270, shorter way round
HL270   HR270turn left / right onto 270 (long way if you say so)
C070   C7000climb: 1–3 digits = flight level, 4–5 digits = feet
D050descend to FL050 (5000 ft)
A090maintain FL090 — climb or descend as required

Approach gate

An ILS clearance is only accepted when the aircraft is inside 12 NM of the threshold, within ±30° of runway heading, within 35° of the extended centreline, at or below the 3° glideslope and at 250 kt or less. Otherwise the pilot tells you why not. Unstable at 3 NM, or a runway still occupied at 2 NM, gets you a go-around.
